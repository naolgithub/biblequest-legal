Common questions

How do I sign in?

BibleQuest uses Sign in with Apple or Google. Use the same one each time, so your progress, XP and streak follow you to every device.

My progress didn't appear on my other phone

Check that you signed in with the same Apple or Google account on both. Progress you made offline is sent the next time you open the app online.

How do streaks work?

Finish a lesson, a review or the daily challenge to keep your streak. A streak freeze covers a day you miss. Lessons never lock because of a streak.

Reminders aren't showing up

Turn reminders on in Profile, and check that notifications for BibleQuest are allowed in your phone's Settings.

How do I add the home-screen widget?

Touch and hold your home screen, tap the + button (Edit, then Add Widget, on newer iPhones), search for BibleQuest and choose a size.

BibleQuest Plus: cancelling, restoring and refunds

BibleQuest Plus is billed by Apple. To cancel, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. On a new phone, tap Restore purchases on the Plus screen. For refunds, visit reportaproblem.apple.com. Cancelling keeps Plus until the end of the period you paid for.

How do I delete my account?

Open Profile and tap Delete account. This removes your account and all of its data straight away. Deleting your account doesn't cancel a Plus subscription; cancel it in Settings first.

Which Bible translation is used?

The World English Bible (WEB), which is in the public domain. It is built into the app, so you can read it offline.

I found a mistake in a lesson

Email us with the lesson's name and what looks wrong. Thank you, it really helps.
