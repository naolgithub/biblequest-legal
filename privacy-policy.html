BibleQuest Privacy Policy

Effective 25 November 2026 · Last updated 25 November 2026

BibleQuest is a Bible learning app made by the BibleQuest team ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who else handles it, and the choices you have. We do not sell your information, we do not show ads, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Summary

  • You sign in with Apple or Google. We receive your name and email address from them.
  • We store your learning progress (lessons, answers, XP, streaks, badges and review schedule) so it is saved across your devices.
  • Some things never leave your phone: your learning preferences, reminder settings, Bible bookmarks, highlights and reading history, and your Ask Lumi conversations.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics or crash-reporting tools.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and all of its data at any time.

Information we collect

Your account

BibleQuest requires you to sign in with Sign in with Apple or Google. When you do, we receive an account identifier, your name and your email address from that provider. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email", we receive a private relay address instead of your real one. We use this information to create your account, to show your name in the app, and to contact you about your account if needed.

Your learning progress

To save your progress and award XP, we store on our servers:

  • the lessons, units and courses you complete, and the answers you give to exercises;
  • your XP, level, badges, streak, streak freezes and the days you were active;
  • your Smart Review schedule, daily challenge results and saved memory verses.

XP, streaks and badges are calculated on our servers from this record.

Notifications

Learning reminders are scheduled on your phone and are only sent if you turn them on. If you allow notifications, we also store your device's push notification token and whether it is an iPhone or an Android phone, so we can tell you about new content. Turning notifications off in your phone's settings stops them.

BibleQuest Plus (iPhone)

If you subscribe to BibleQuest Plus, payment is handled entirely by Apple. We never see or store your card or bank details. Our subscription provider, RevenueCat, tells us whether your subscription is active, which plan you have, when it renews or ends, and whether you are in a free trial.

Ask Lumi (AI tutor)

When Ask Lumi is available and you use it, your question and details of the lesson or Bible passage you are asking about are sent through our servers to OpenAI, which writes the answer. We ask OpenAI not to store the conversation. Questions are also checked by OpenAI's automated safety filter. Your Ask Lumi conversations are saved only on your phone. On our servers we keep only a count of how many questions you asked each day, to apply the daily limit. If you report an answer, we store that question, the answer, the reason you chose and any note you add, so we can review and improve Lumi.

Please don't include personal details, such as your address or health information, in questions to Lumi.

Information that stays on your phone

The following are stored only on your device and are not sent to us:

  • your onboarding answers and learning preferences;
  • reminder, sound and appearance settings;
  • Bible bookmarks, highlights and reading history;
  • Ask Lumi conversations;
  • the data shown in the home-screen widget.

Read aloud uses your phone's built-in voice, and the text does not leave your device. Deleting the app removes this information.

How we use information

We use the information above only to:

  • run the app: sign you in, save your progress, keep your streak and review schedule, and send the notifications you allow;
  • provide BibleQuest Plus to subscribers;
  • answer Ask Lumi questions and review reported answers;
  • keep the service secure and prevent abuse;
  • respond when you contact us.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of our users or others. If BibleQuest is ever sold or transferred, this policy will continue to apply to your information.

Where information is stored

Our servers are run by Supabase in the United States (Virginia). Our providers may process information in other countries. Where the law requires it, we rely on appropriate safeguards for those transfers.

How long we keep information

We keep your account and learning progress for as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, progress, push tokens, subscription status and any reported Ask Lumi answers from our servers within 30 days. Copies in backups are removed when those backups expire, within 90 days. Apple and RevenueCat keep their own purchase records under their policies.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: email us at [CONTACT EMAIL] from the address linked to your account, and we will delete it and all of its data.
  • Access or correct your information: email us and we will send you a copy of the information we hold, or correct it.
  • Notifications: turn reminders off in Profile, or turn notifications off in your phone's settings.
  • Subscriptions: manage or cancel BibleQuest Plus in your Apple ID settings.
  • Sign in with Apple: you can stop BibleQuest using your Apple ID in your iPhone's Settings → [your name] → Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple.

Depending on where you live, you may have further rights, for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK or the CCPA in California. These can include the right to object to or restrict processing, to receive your data in a portable format, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Where the GDPR applies, we process your information to provide the service you asked for (performance of a contract), to keep it secure (legitimate interests), and, for notifications, with your consent. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

Children

BibleQuest is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Information is encrypted while it travels between the app and our servers. Access to each person's data is restricted at the database level, so an account can only read its own progress. Keys for our providers are kept on our servers, not in the app. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will tell you if a breach affects you, as the law requires.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If a change is significant, for example if we start collecting a new kind of information, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
